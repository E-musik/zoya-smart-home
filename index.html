About Zoya Smart Home

Automation is all about leveraging technology to the benefit of end users, making life easier, safer and more convenient. At Zoya Smart Homes, we see automation as an avenue to touch lives and enhance living in meaningful ways. We aren't a regular home automation company, because technology in itself is not limited, rather we grow every day with new ideas that are continuously implemented to further satisfy our cherished clients and push the minds of our ever creative group of teams. At Zoya, right from inception we believe strongly in not being limited, thereby growing a team that aren't constricted by a box to think out of. We are deeply committed to creating an indelible mark in the evolving world of the Internet of Things and wireless technologies, mostly for the benefits of our end users. Instead of just the procurement and assembling of instruments, we focused on the manufacture and development of automation products and solutions
